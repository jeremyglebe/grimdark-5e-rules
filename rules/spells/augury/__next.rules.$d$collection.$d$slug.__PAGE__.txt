3:T4c0,<p><em>Level 2 Divination (Cleric, Druid, Wizard)</em></p>
<p><strong>Casting Time:</strong> 1 minute or Ritual
<strong>Range:</strong> Self
<strong>Components:</strong> V, S, M (specially marked sticks, bones, cards, or other divinatory tokens worth 25+ GP)
<strong>Duration:</strong> Instantaneous</p>
<p>You receive an omen from an otherworldly entity about the results of a course of action that you plan to take within the next 30 minutes. The GM chooses the omen from the Omens table.</p>
<p><strong>Omens</strong></p>
<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Omen</th>
      <th>For Results That Will Be …</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Weal</td>
      <td>Good</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Woe</td>
      <td>Bad</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Weal and woe</td>
      <td>Good and bad</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Indifference</td>
      <td>Neither good nor bad</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table><p>The spell doesn't account for circumstances, such as other spells, that might change the results.</p>
<p>If you cast the spell more than once before finishing a Long Rest, there is a cumulative 25 percent chance for each casting after the first that you get no answer.</p>
