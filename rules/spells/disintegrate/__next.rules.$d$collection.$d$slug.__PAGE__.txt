3:T456,<p><em>Level 6 Transmutation (Sorcerer, Wizard)</em></p>
<p><strong>Casting Time:</strong> Action
<strong>Range:</strong> 60 feet
<strong>Components:</strong> V, S, M (a lodestone and dust)
<strong>Duration:</strong> Instantaneous</p>
<p>You launch a green ray at a target you can see within range. The target can be a creature, a nonmagical object, or a creation of magical force, such as the wall created by <em>Wall of Force</em>.</p>
<p>A creature targeted by this spell makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 10d6 + 40 Force damage. If this damage reduces it to 0 Hit Points, it and everything nonmagical it is wearing and carrying are disintegrated into gray dust. The target can be revived only by a <em>True Resurrection</em> or a <em>Wish</em> spell.</p>
<p>This spell automatically disintegrates a Large or smaller nonmagical object or a creation of magical force. If such a target is Huge or larger, this spell disintegrates a 10-foot-Cube portion of it.</p>
<p><em>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.</em> The damage increases by 3d6 for each spell slot level above 6.</p>
