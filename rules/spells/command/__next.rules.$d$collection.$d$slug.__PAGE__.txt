3:T43e,<p><em>Level 1 Enchantment (Bard, Cleric, Paladin)</em></p>
<p><strong>Casting Time:</strong> Action
<strong>Range:</strong> 60 feet
<strong>Components:</strong> V
<strong>Duration:</strong> Instantaneous</p>
<p>You speak a one-word command to a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or follow the command on its next turn. Choose the command from these options:</p>
<p><strong>Approach.</strong> The target moves toward you by the shortest and most direct route, ending its turn if it moves within 5 feet of you.</p>
<p><strong>Drop.</strong> The target drops whatever it is holding and then ends its turn.</p>
<p><strong>Flee.</strong> The target spends its turn moving away from you by the fastest available means.</p>
<p><strong>Grovel.</strong> The target has the Prone condition and then ends its turn.</p>
<p><strong>Halt.</strong> On its turn, the target doesn't move and takes no action or Bonus Action.</p>
<p><em>Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.</em> You can affect one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1.</p>
