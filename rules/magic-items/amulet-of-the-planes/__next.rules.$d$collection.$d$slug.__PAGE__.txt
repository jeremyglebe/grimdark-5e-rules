3:T634,<p><em>Wondrous Item, Very Rare (Requires Attunement)</em></p>
<p>While wearing this amulet, you can take a Magic action to name a location that you are familiar with on another plane of existence. Then make a DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check. On a successful check, you cast <em>Plane Shift</em>. On a failed check, you and each creature and object within 15 feet of you travel to a random destination determined by rolling 1d100 and consulting the following table.</p>
