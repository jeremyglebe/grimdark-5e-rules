10:T5a4,<p><em>Weapon (Quarterstaff), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)</em></p>
<p>You have a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.</p>
<p>While holding this weapon, you can cause it to emit green Dim Light out to 10 feet, either as a Bonus Action or after you roll Initiative, or you can extinguish the light as a Bonus Action.</p>
<p>While holding this weapon, you can take a Bonus Action to alter its form, turning it into a 6-inch rod (for ease of storage) or a 10-foot pole, or reverting it a Quarterstaff; the weapon will elongate only as far as the surrounding space allows.</p>
<p>In certain forms, the weapon has the following additional properties.</p>
<p><em>Acrobatic Assist (Quarterstaff and 10-Foot Pole Forms Only).</em> While holding this weapon, you have Advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.</p>
<p><em>Attack Deflection (Quarterstaff Form Only).</em> When you are hit by an attack while holding the weapon, you can take a Reaction to twirl the weapon around you, gaining a +5 bonus to your Armor Class against the triggering attack, potentially causing the attack to miss you. You can't use this property again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.</p>
<p><em>Ranged Weapon (Quarterstaff Form Only).</em> This weapon has the Thrown property with a normal range of 30 feet and a long range of 120 feet. Immediately after you make a ranged attack with the weapon, it flies back to your hand.</p>
