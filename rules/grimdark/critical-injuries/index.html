Changes these 2024 rules:Damage and HealingCritical Hit

Replaces: critical hits as merely doubled damage dice.

A Critical Injury is a serious physical wound determined on an appropriate injury table. It is distinct from Hit Point loss and can have lasting consequences.

An attacker's natural 20 inflicts a Critical Injury but does not double its damage dice. A defender's natural 20 uses the selected response: Parry injures the attacker, Dodge negates damage, and Tank the Hit cannot produce a defensive natural 20 because no roll occurs.

The working severity roll is d100 + Excess Damage + 10 per existing Critical Injury. The +10 escalation is a draft value.

When a natural 20, Surprise, and Excess Damage provide multiple Critical Injury triggers on the same blow, resolve one Critical Injury using the Excess Damage modifier rather than causing separate injuries. A Parry natural 20 and attacker natural 20 can cause mutual Critical Injuries because they affect different creatures.

The interaction between an attacker's natural 20 and a Dodging defender's natural 20 remains open.

Grimdark 5e material: an unofficial modification or addition to the SRD 5.2.1 baseline.
