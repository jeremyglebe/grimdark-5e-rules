10:T63f,<p><strong>Replaces:</strong> critical hits as merely doubled damage dice.</p>
<p>A Critical Injury is a serious wound determined on the table for its damage type. It is distinct from Hit Point loss and can have lasting consequences. The thirteen tables cover Acid, Bludgeoning, Cold, Fire, Force, Lightning, Necrotic, Piercing, Poison, Psychic, Radiant, Slashing, and Thunder damage without a separate hit-location roll.</p>
<p>An attacker's natural 20 inflicts a Critical Injury but does not double its damage dice. A defender's natural 20 uses the selected response: Parry injures the attacker, Dodge negates damage, and Tank the Hit cannot produce a defensive natural 20 because no roll occurs.</p>
<p>The working severity roll is <code>d100 + Excess Damage + 10 per existing Critical Injury</code>. The +10 escalation is a draft value.</p>
<p>Modified results can exceed 100. Results of 116 or higher are fatal unless Fate or an appropriate effect says otherwise. The table rules define how to choose a type for mixed damage, resolve anatomy from the fiction, track Bleeding, perform treatment, recover naturally, and meet magical-healing thresholds.</p>
<p>When a natural 20, Surprise, and Excess Damage provide multiple Critical Injury triggers on the same blow, resolve one Critical Injury using the Excess Damage modifier rather than causing separate injuries. A Parry natural 20 and attacker natural 20 can cause mutual Critical Injuries because they affect different creatures.</p>
<p>The interaction between an attacker's natural 20 and a Dodging defender's natural 20 remains open.</p>
