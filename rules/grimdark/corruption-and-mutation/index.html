Corruption and Mutation

Corruption tracks exposure to Chaos, forbidden magic, tainted places, cursed objects, and other transformative forces. Corrupting Exposure has a severity, and characters may resist, accumulate Corruption, or suffer a Mutation.

Mutation is a consequence rather than a cosmetic choice. It can be physical, mental, spiritual, beneficial in one respect while harmful in another, or socially dangerous. Resolve may allow a character to resist or reject a Mutation.

Exposure ratings, thresholds, resistance tests, Mutation tables, and removal procedures remain to be defined.

Grimdark 5e material: an unofficial modification or addition to the SRD 5.2.1 baseline.
