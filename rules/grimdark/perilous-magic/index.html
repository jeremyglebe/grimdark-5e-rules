Changes these 2024 rules:Casting Spells

Adds to and modifies: the 2024 spellcasting rules.

Spell descriptions, spell levels, components, ranges, durations, concentration, and ordinary spell-slot casting remain the baseline unless a Grimdark rule changes them. Spellcasting becomes less routine through Strain, Miscasts, Raw Casting, magical traditions, and corrupting consequences.

The module should preserve compatibility with existing spells while making dangerous choices explicit before dice are rolled.

Grimdark 5e material: an unofficial modification or addition to the SRD 5.2.1 baseline.
