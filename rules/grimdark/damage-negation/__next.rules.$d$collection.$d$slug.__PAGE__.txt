3:T646,<p><strong>Replaces or modifies:</strong> Armor Class, parts of Resistance, and the ordinary damage application procedure.</p>
<p><code>Damage Negation = applicable Toughness + Armour + Shield + situational modifiers</code></p>
<p>When Tanking the Hit, also add the defender's Strength modifier for that hit. A negative Strength modifier is not clamped: choosing to Tank with one reduces total Negation and can make the hit hurt more. Subtract the final Damage Negation once from the entire hit or effect, and then apply the remaining Hit Point loss.</p>
<p>By default, Toughness, Armour, Shield, and Tank Negation apply to every damage type. <strong>Force damage ignores all Damage Negation</strong>, representing direct pure magical damage. When one hit deals Force and non-Force damage, only the Force portion bypasses; combine the remaining damage and apply Negation to that remainder once. Individual effects can define other exceptions. Armour Piercing is reserved for later development; no weapons currently have it.</p>
<p>An attack that hits normally causes at least 1 Hit Point loss after Negation unless it is deliberately pulled, nonlethal, or a rule explicitly says it can be reduced to 0.</p>
<p>Apply every rule that changes the damage itself—including Resistance, Vulnerability, Immunity, and the Resistance granted by features such as Barbarian Rage—before Damage Negation. The resulting amount is incoming damage. Negation does not change that incoming damage; it reduces the Hit Points lost from it. Damage-changing rules resolve first; Hit Point-loss reduction resolves second.</p>
