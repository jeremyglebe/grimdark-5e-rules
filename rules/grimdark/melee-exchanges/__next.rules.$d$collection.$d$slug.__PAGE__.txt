3:T4dd,<p><strong>Replaces:</strong> ordinary melee attack rolls against a static Armor Class.</p>
<p>A melee attack is an opposed exchange when the defender is able and willing to respond.</p>
<ol>
<li>The attacker declares the target, weapon, and any option that must be chosen before rolling.</li>
<li>The defender chooses <strong>Parry</strong>, <strong>Dodge</strong>, or <strong>Tank the Hit</strong>, or the GM identifies the attack as <strong>Unopposed</strong> or <strong>Surprised</strong>.</li>
<li>Roll the attack and any opposed defense. Determine the winner and any natural-20 response effect.</li>
<li>Update Momentum if the exchange was opposed.</li>
<li>If the attack deals damage, roll damage and recursively explode maximum damage dice.</li>
<li>Apply Damage Negation once, reduce Hit Points, calculate Excess Damage, and resolve any Critical Injury.</li>
</ol>
<p>The attacker rolls <code>d20 + the normal melee attack bonus</code>. Parry and Dodge use their own formulas. The higher total wins. The winning side gains 1 Momentum when the exchange was opposed.</p>
<p>Defenders do not deal normal weapon damage merely for winning a defense unless a feature specifically says otherwise. The former Riposte rule has been removed.</p>
