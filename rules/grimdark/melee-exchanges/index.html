Changes these 2024 rules:Combat

Replaces: ordinary melee attack rolls against a static Armor Class.

A melee attack is an opposed exchange when the defender is able and willing to respond.

  1. The attacker declares the target, weapon, and any option that must be chosen before rolling.
  2. The defender chooses Parry, Dodge, or Tank the Hit, or the GM identifies the attack as Unopposed or Surprised.
  3. Roll the attack and any opposed defense. Determine the winner and any natural-20 response effect.
  4. Update Momentum if the exchange was opposed.
  5. If the attack deals damage, roll damage and recursively explode maximum damage dice.
  6. Apply Damage Negation once, reduce Hit Points, calculate Excess Damage, and resolve any Critical Injury.

The attacker rolls d20 + the normal melee attack bonus. Parry and Dodge use their own formulas. The higher total wins. The winning side gains 1 Momentum when the exchange was opposed.

Defenders do not deal normal weapon damage merely for winning a defense unless a feature specifically says otherwise. The former Riposte rule has been removed.

Grimdark 5e material: an unofficial modification or addition to the SRD 5.2.1 baseline.
