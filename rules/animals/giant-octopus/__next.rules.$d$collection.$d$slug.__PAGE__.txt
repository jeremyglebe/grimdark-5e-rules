<h3>Traits</h3>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Water Breathing.</em></strong> The octopus can breathe only underwater. It can hold its breath for 1 hour outside water.</p>
<h3>Actions</h3>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Tentacles.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +5, reach 10 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13) from all eight tentacles. While Grappled, the target has the Restrained condition.</p>
<h3>Reactions</h3>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Ink Cloud (1/Day).</em></strong> <em>Trigger:</em> The octopus takes damage while underwater. <em>Response:</em> The octopus releases ink that fills a 10-foot Cube centered on itself, and the octopus moves up to its Swim Speed. The Cube is Heavily Obscured for 1 minute or until a strong current or similar effect disperses the ink.</p>
