<h3>Traits</h3>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Spider Climb.</em></strong> If the swarm has a Climb Speed, the swarm can climb difficult surfaces, including along ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.</p>
<p><strong><em>Swarm.</em></strong> The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and the swarm can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny insect. The swarm can't regain Hit Points or gain Temporary Hit Points.</p>
