<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Ephemeral.</em></strong> The wisp can't wear or carry anything.</p>
<p><strong><em>Illumination.</em></strong> The wisp sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet.</p>
<p><strong><em>Incorporeal Movement.</em></strong> The wisp can move through other creatures and objects as if they were Difficult Terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) Force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.</p>

<hr /><p><strong><em>Shock.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +4, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 11 (2d8 + 2) Lightning damage.</p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Consume Life.</em></strong> <em>Constitution Saving Throw:</em> DC 10, one living creature the wisp can see within 5 feet that has 0 Hit Points. <em>Failure:</em> The target dies, and the wisp regains 10 (3d6) Hit Points.</p>
