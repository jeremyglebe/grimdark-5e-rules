<h4>Traits</h4>

<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Pseudopod.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +4, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 12 (3d6 + 2) Acid damage.</p>
<h4>Reactions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Split.</em></strong> <em>Trigger:</em> While the jelly is Large or Medium and has 10+ Hit Points, it becomes Bloodied or is subjected to Lightning or Slashing damage. <em>Response:</em> The jelly splits into two new <strong>Ochre Jellies</strong>. Each new jelly is one size smaller than the original jelly and acts on its Initiative. The original jelly's Hit Points are divided evenly between the new jellies (round down).</p>
