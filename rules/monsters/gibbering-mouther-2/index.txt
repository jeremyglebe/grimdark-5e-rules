<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Aberrant Ground.</em></strong> The ground in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the mouther is Difficult Terrain.</p>
<p><strong><em>Gibbering.</em></strong> The mouther babbles incoherently while it doesn't have the Incapacitated condition. <em>Wisdom Saving Throw:</em> DC 10, any creature that starts its turn within 20 feet of the mouther while it is babbling. <em>Failure:</em> The target rolls 1d8 to determine what it does during the current turn: <br />
 <strong>1–4.</strong> The target does nothing.<br />
 <strong>5–6.</strong> The target takes no action or Bonus Action and uses all its movement to move in a random direction.<br />
 <strong>7–8.</strong> The target makes a melee attack against a randomly determined creature within its reach or does nothing if it can't make such an attack.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Bite.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +2, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 7 (2d6) Piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition. The target dies if it is reduced to 0 Hit Points by this attack. Its body is then absorbed into the mouther, leaving only equipment behind.</p>
<p><strong><em>Blinding Spittle (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> <em>Dexterity Saving Throw:</em> DC 10, each creature in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point within 30 feet. <em>Failure:</em> 7 (2d6) Radiant damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of the mouther's next turn.</p>
