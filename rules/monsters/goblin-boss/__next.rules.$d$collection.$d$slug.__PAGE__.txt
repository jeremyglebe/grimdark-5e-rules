<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Nimble Escape.</em></strong> The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.</p>
<h4>Reactions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Redirect Attack.</em></strong> <em>Trigger:</em> A creature the goblin can see makes an attack roll against it. <em>Response:</em> The goblin chooses a Small or Medium ally within 5 feet of itself.
