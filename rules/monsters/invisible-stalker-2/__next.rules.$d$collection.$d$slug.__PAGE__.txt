<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Air Form.</em></strong> The stalker can enter an enemy's space and stop there. It can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.</p>
<p><strong><em>Invisibility.</em></strong> The stalker has the Invisible condition.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The stalker makes three Wind Swipe attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Vortex.</p>
<p><strong><em>Wind Swipe.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +7, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 11 (2d6 + 4) Force damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Vortex.</em></strong> <em>Constitution Saving Throw:</em> DC 14, one Large or smaller creature in the stalker's space. <em>Failure:</em> 7 (1d8 + 3) Thunder damage, and the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13).
