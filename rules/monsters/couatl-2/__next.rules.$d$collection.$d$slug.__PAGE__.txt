<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Shielded Mind.</em></strong> The couatl's thoughts can't be read by any means, and other creatures can communicate with it telepathically only if it allows them.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Bite.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +7, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 11 (1d12 + 5) Piercing damage, and the target has the Poisoned condition until the end of the couatl's next turn.</p>
<p><strong><em>Constrict.</em></strong> <em>Strength Saving Throw:</em> DC 15, one Medium or smaller creature the couatl can see within 5 feet. <em>Failure:</em> 8 (1d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage. The target has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13), and it has the Restrained condition until the grapple ends.</p>
<p><strong><em>Spellcasting.</em></strong> The couatl casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15): <br />
 <strong>At Will:</strong> <em>Detect Evil and Good, Detect Magic, Detect Thoughts, Shapechange</em> (Beast or Humanoid form only, no Temporary Hit Points gained from the spell, and no Concentration or Temporary Hit Points required to maintain the spell) <br />
 <strong>1/Day Each:</strong> <em>Create Food and Water</em>, <em>Dream</em>, <em>Greater Restoration</em>, <em>Scrying</em>, <em>Sleep</em></p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Divine Aid (2/Day).</em></strong> The couatl casts <em>Bless</em>, <em>Lesser Restoration</em>, or <em>Sanctuary</em>, requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.</p>
