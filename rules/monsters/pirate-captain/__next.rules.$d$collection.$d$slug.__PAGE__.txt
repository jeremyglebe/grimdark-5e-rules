<hr /><p><strong><em>Captain's Charm.</em></strong> <em>Wisdom Saving Throw:</em> DC 14, one creature the pirate can see within 30 feet. <em>Failure:</em> The target has the Charmed condition until the start of the pirate's next turn.</p>

<hr /><p><strong><em>Riposte.</em></strong> <em>Trigger:</em> The pirate is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon. <em>Response:</em> The pirate adds 3 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
