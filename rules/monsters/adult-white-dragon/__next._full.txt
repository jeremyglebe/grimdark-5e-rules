<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Ice Walk.</em></strong> The dragon can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, Difficult Terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra movement.</p>
<p><strong><em>Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).</em></strong> If the dragon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The dragon makes three Rend attacks.</p>
<p><strong><em>Rend.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +11, reach 10 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 13 (2d6 + 6) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Cold Breath (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> <em>Constitution Saving Throw:</em> DC 19, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. <em>Failure:</em> 54 (12d8) Cold damage. <em>Success:</em> Half damage.</p>
<h4>Legendary Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><em>Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the dragon can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The dragon regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.</em></p>
<p><strong><em>Freezing Burst.</em></strong> <em>Constitution Saving Throw:</em> DC 14, each creature in a 30-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the dragon can see within 120 feet. <em>Failure:</em> 7 (2d6) Cold damage, and the target's Speed is 0 until the end of the target's next turn. <em>Failure or Success:</em> The dragon can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.</p>
<p><strong><em>Frightful Presence.</em></strong> The dragon casts <em>Fear</em>, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14). The dragon can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.</p>
<p><strong><em>Pounce.</em></strong> The dragon moves up to half its Speed, and it makes one Rend attack.</p>
