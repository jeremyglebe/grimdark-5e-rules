<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Divine Awareness.</em></strong> The planetar knows if it hears a lie.</p>
<p><strong><em>Exalted Restoration.</em></strong> If the planetar dies outside Mount Celestia, its body disappears, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its Hit Points somewhere in Mount Celestia.</p>
<p><strong><em>Magic Resistance.</em></strong> The planetar has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The planetar makes three Radiant Sword attacks or uses Holy Burst twice.</p>
<p><strong><em>Radiant Sword.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +12, reach 10 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 14 (2d6 + 7) Slashing damage plus 18 (4d8) Radiant damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Holy Burst.</em></strong> <em>Dexterity Saving Throw:</em> DC 20, each enemy in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point the planetar can see within 120 feet. <em>Failure:</em> 24 (7d6) Radiant damage. <em>Success:</em> Half damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Spellcasting.</em></strong> The planetar casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as spellcasting ability (spell save DC 20): <br />
 <strong>At Will:</strong> <em>Detect Evil and Good</em> <br />
 <strong>1/Day Each:</strong> <em>Commune</em>, <em>Control Weather</em>, <em>Dispel Evil and Good</em>, <em>Raise Dead</em></p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Divine Aid (2/Day).</em></strong> The planetar casts <em>Cure Wounds</em>, <em>Invisibility</em>, <em>Lesser Restoration</em>, or <em>Remove Curse</em>, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.</p>
