<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Amorphous.</em></strong> The shadow can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so.</p>
<p><strong><em>Sunlight Weakness.</em></strong> While in sunlight, the shadow has Disadvantage on D20 Tests.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Draining Swipe.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +4, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 5 (1d6 + 2) Necrotic damage, and the target's Strength score decreases by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces that score to 0. If a Humanoid is slain by this attack, a <strong>Shadow</strong> rises from the corpse 1d4 hours later.</p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Shadow Stealth.</em></strong> While in Dim Light or Darkness, the shadow takes the Hide action.</p>
