<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Lair).</em></strong> If the dragon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The dragon makes three Rend attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of (A) Paralyzing Breath or (B) Spellcasting to cast <em>Ice Knife</em>.</p>
<p><strong><em>Rend.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +13, reach 10 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 17 (2d8 + 8) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Cold damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Cold Breath (Recharge 5–6).</em></strong> <em>Constitution Saving Throw:</em> DC 20, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. <em>Failure:</em> 54 (12d8) Cold damage. <em>Success:</em> Half damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Paralyzing Breath.</em></strong> <em>Constitution Saving Throw:</em> DC 20, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. <em>First Failure:</em> The target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn, when it repeats the save. <em>Second Failure:</em> The target has the Paralyzed condition, and it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. After 1 minute, it succeeds automatically.</p>
<p><strong><em>Spellcasting.</em></strong> The dragon casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19, +11 to hit with spell attacks):<br />
 <strong>At Will:</strong> <em>Detect Magic</em>, <em>Hold Monster</em>, <em>Ice Knife</em>, <em>Shapechange</em> (Beast or Humanoid form only, no Temporary Hit Points gained from the spell, and no Concentration or Temporary Hit Points required to maintain the spell) <br />
 <strong>1/Day Each:</strong> <em>Ice Storm</em> (level 5 version), <em>Zone of Truth</em></p>
<h4>Legendary Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><em>Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature's turn, the dragon can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The dragon regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.</em></p>
<p><strong><em>Chill.</em></strong> The dragon uses Spellcasting to cast <em>Hold Monster</em>. The dragon can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.</p>
<p><strong><em>Cold Gale.</em></strong> <em>Dexterity Saving Throw:</em> DC 19, each creature in a 60-foot-long, 10-foot-wide Line. <em>Failure:</em> 14 (4d6) Cold damage, and the target is pushed up to 30 feet straight away from the dragon. <em>Success:</em> Half damage only. <em>Failure or Success:</em> The dragon can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.</p>
<p><strong><em>Pounce.</em></strong> The dragon moves up to half its Speed, and it makes one Rend attack.</p>
