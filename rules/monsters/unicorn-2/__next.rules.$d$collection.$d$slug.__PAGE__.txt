<strong>Languages</strong> Celestial, Elvish, Sylvan; telepathy 120 ft. <br />

<h4>Traits</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Legendary Resistance (3/Day).</em></strong> If the unicorn fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.</p>
<p><strong><em>Magic Resistance.</em></strong> The unicorn has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.</p>
<h4>Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The unicorn makes one Hooves attack and one Radiant Horn attack.</p>
<p><strong><em>Hooves.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +7, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 11 (2d6 + 4) Bludgeoning damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Radiant Horn.</em></strong> <em>Melee Attack Roll:</em> +7, reach 5 ft. <em>Hit:</em> 9 (1d10 + 4) Radiant damage.</p>
<p><strong><em>Spellcasting.</em></strong> The unicorn casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14): <br />
 <strong>At Will:</strong> <em>Detect Evil and Good, Druidcraft</em> <br />
 <strong>1/Day Each:</strong> <em>Calm Emotions, Dispel Evil and Good, Entangle, Pass without Trace, Word of Recall</em></p>
<h4>Bonus Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><strong><em>Unicorn's Blessing (3/Day).</em></strong> The unicorn touches another creature with its horn and casts <em>Cure Wounds</em> or <em>Lesser Restoration</em> on that creature, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.</p>
<h4>Legendary Actions</h4>
<hr /><p><em>Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, the unicorn can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The unicorn regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.</em></p>
<p><strong><em>Charging Horn.</em></strong> The unicorn moves up to half its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks, and it makes one Radiant Horn attack.</p>
<p><strong><em>Shimmering Shield.</em></strong> The unicorn targets itself or one creature it can see within 60 feet of itself. The target gains 10 (3d6) Temporary Hit Points, and its AC increases by 2 until the end of the unicorn's next turn. <br />
 The unicorn can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.</p>
