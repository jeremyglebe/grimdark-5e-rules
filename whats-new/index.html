For revised 2024 5e players

What’s new in
Grimdark 5e?

The short version: familiar characters and spells, but every violent or magical choice carries more physical weight.

2024 rule retainedGrimdark replacement or addition
01

Melee becomes an exchange

Instead of swinging against static AC, attacker and defender contest the moment. The defender chooses whether to Parry, Dodge, absorb the blow, or make no defense at all.

Melee Exchanges →Defense Responses →Momentum →
02

Armour absorbs damage

Dexterity moves into active dodging. Armour, shields, endurance, and sometimes Strength combine into Damage Negation after a hit lands.

Armour & Shields →Toughness →Damage Negation →
03

Damage can always surprise you

Every damage die explodes when it rolls its maximum. Weak attacks can spike, but broad bonus-dice interactions are still being playtested.

Exploding Damage Dice →Damage Procedure →
04

Criticals cause injuries

A natural 20 no longer merely doubles dice. It creates a concrete Critical Injury selected from a full table for the attack’s damage type; Parry and Dodge have their own distinct natural-20 effects.

Critical Injuries →Critical Injury Tables →Defense Responses →
05

Zero HP is Mortal Peril

Characters do not automatically fall unconscious and do not use ordinary death saves. Excess Damage drives injury severity, and death follows from actual wounds.

Mortal Peril →Healing Critical Injuries →
06

Magic carries risk

Normal spell text stays useful, but Raw Casting, Strain, Miscasts, traditions, and corrupting consequences make magical power a consequential choice.

Perilous Magic →Raw Casting & Strain →Miscasts →
07

Heroes survive by more than HP

Fate can avert a final outcome. Resolve resists terror, domination, mutation, and debilitating consequences. Both are intentionally scarce.

Fate →Resolve →Fear & Terror →
08

The world leaves marks

Chaos exposure, Corruption, Mutation, career backgrounds, and creature conversions build setting consequences directly into the familiar 5e structure.

Corruption & Mutation →Career Backgrounds →Bestiary Framework →

Still in design

Draft values stay visible.

Resistance, large exploding dice pools, armour-piercing values, Critical Injury tuning, and several resource scales remain open. The site marks them rather than presenting guesses as settled rules.
